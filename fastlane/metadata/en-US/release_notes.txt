A major sTalk update: Drive, search, channels, meetings and calls.

Drive and files
• Drive file cards in chats, support for internal and external links, and document opening from conversations.
• Expanded document and photo search, photo albums and favourites.
• Fixed sharing from other apps, including chat selection and preservation of original attachment names.

Search and Ailock
• A dedicated Search app for messages, files, meetings, call recordings and tasks.
• Standard search and smart search with Ailock, voice input and result filters.
• Search history grouped by date, with result counts by content type.
• Updated file cards showing file type, name and author without unnecessary document excerpts.

Chats and channels
• Channels with posts, discussions and participant permissions.
• Create chats, groups and channels from the same screen.
• Filters for people, groups, channels and meetings, with a separate favourites section.
• Updated message menus. Swipe right to left to reply directly to a selected message.
• Discussions in groups and channels, and a simpler interface for direct chats.
• Automatic reconnection and sync status in the chat list header.
• Updated stickers and emoji.

Meetings and calls
• Meeting room selection and booking, room photos, booking rules and check-in.
• Improved participant layouts and restoration of your preferred call view.
• Turn off incoming participant video while keeping audio and screen sharing.
• Updated call history, participant details, summaries and transcripts.
• Linked TrackIT tasks display their numbers and statuses; call results are available in the conversation.
• Fixed meeting details loading errors and other stability issues.
